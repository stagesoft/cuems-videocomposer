/etc/cuems/display.conf:

  # Authored by cuems-display-setup on 2026-05-15T20:50:54
  canvas_layout=custom

  [output:HDMI-A-1]
  canvas_region=0,0,3840,2160
  resolution=3840x2160

  [output:HDMI-A-2]
  canvas_region=3840,0,3840,2160
  resolution=3840x2160

  [output:HDMI-A-3]
  canvas_region=7680,0,1920,1080
  resolution=1920x1080

/etc/cuems/videocomposer-flags.env:

  OPERATOR_FLAGS=--verbose --resolution native

Effect: --resolution native forces DRM mode to EDID native (4K on 4K
panels, 1080p on the 1080p panel), overriding any per-output
resolution= in display.conf. Confirmed by journal WARNING line:
"DRMBackend: Ignoring per-output resolutions from display.conf —
overridden by explicit -r flag".
